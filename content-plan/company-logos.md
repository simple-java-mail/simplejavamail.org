# Fictional case-study company logos

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the Simple Java Mail case studies. These are fictional identities, not customer endorsements. The frontend-design skill guided their distinct styles and restrained placement on the website.

The originals below are transparent 2172 × 724 PNGs (3:1), copied without modification from the generated outputs. The optional `caseStudy.logo` front matter field supplies both the card wordmark and the article masthead; the company name remains their accessible text.

## Generation prompts

### staple-and-sons

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one final production-ready fictional company logo for a developer case-study website, not a mockup or a sheet of options. Company: Staple & Sons, a small established office-supply and shelving wholesaler run by a modest hands-on team. The identity should look believable on its delivery van, invoice and warehouse sign: dependable, practical and unpretentious, with a little family-business warmth.
Design: a simple sturdy squared staple / shelving-bracket emblem beside a confident, carefully spaced slab-serif wordmark. Exact text, once only: "Staple & Sons". No slogan, establishment date or other words. Dark forest green (#285440) ink, flat colors. Avoid distressed vintage effects, elaborate heraldry, mail/envelope symbols, and similarity to any famous office-supply brand. This is an entirely invented company.
Output: a clean horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Wide roughly 3:1 canvas, logo comfortably filling it with a small consistent safety margin on all sides. Crisp vector-like edges, balanced negative space, no gradients, shadows, 3D, photograph, signage mockup, watermarks, or checkerboard pattern. Wordmark must remain legible at 260 CSS pixels wide on white.
```

Friendly, competent and modern, with a clean product-design sensibility.

Output: a horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent alpha background, wide roughly 3:1 canvas. Logo comfortably fills it with a small consistent safety margin. Balanced symbol and wordmark, crisp vector-like edges, flat colors, strong recognizable silhouette. No gradients, shadows, 3D, photo or app mockup, watermarks, or checkerboard pattern.
